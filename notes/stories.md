# Stories (STAR: situation, task, action, result)

Aim for 5–6, each under two minutes, with a number in the result.

1. **Biggest outage I handled** — staying composed under pressure.
2. **Automation that removed toil across the fleet** — ownership, scale (~2,000 servers).
3. **A hard root cause** — systems troubleshooting (kernel, network, disk, hardware).
4. **A risky change rolled out safely** — patching/upgrades without downtime (maps to cluster upgrades).
5. **Working with a difficult stakeholder** — e.g. a trading or quant team.
6. **Juggling priorities** — incident vs. project work.
